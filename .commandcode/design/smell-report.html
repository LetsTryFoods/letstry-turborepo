Animated gradients + a single navy hue

#0C5273 is the entire color identity on a warm, roast-y snack brand. The Healthy Snacking section stacks a 3-color background gradient under a shine-animated orange gradient heading — two moving gradients on one screen.

FIX: /design recolor — a palette that tastes like the food (warm browns, turmeric, chilli reds); zero animated gradients.

Centered everything, type with no system

Headlines jump between text-xl, text-2xl, text-3xl, text-4xl and inline clamp() values with no shared scale. Category, healthy, testimonials, and hero all center their headlines over equal cards. The page reads as a stack of identical sections.

FIX: /design relayout + /design typeset — alternate composition per section; one scale, one measure, one rhythm.
P1

Toy motion and celebration energy

Confetti fires on bestseller scroll, three carousels autoplay, every add-to-cart fires a spark burst, ornaments sway continuously, and the sale strip reuses the emoji-and-gradient energy. Constant celebration flattens the moments that matter.

FIX: /design motion — one entrance system, restrained; no autoplay on three carousels, no confetti, no spark on every add.

Equal testimonial cards with icon avatars

A uniform grid of bordered cards, each with an icon avatar circle, title, text, name. Nothing ranks, no verified badge, no lead testimonial.

FIX: /design relayout — lead with one strong testimonial; let the rest follow.
